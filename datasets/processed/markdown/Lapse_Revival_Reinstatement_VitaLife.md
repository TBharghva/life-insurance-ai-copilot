## LAPSE, REVIVAL &amp; REINSTATEMENT GUIDE

VitaLife Insurance Corporation | Customer Service | 2025 Edition

## PART 1: POLICY LAPSE

## What Is a Lapse?

A policy lapses when the premium due is not paid within the grace period. The grace period is 30 days for annual, semi-annual, and quarterly premiums, and 15 days for monthly premiums.

CONSEQUENCES OF LAPSE BY PRODUCT TYPE:

TERM LIFE (VitaShield  Term):  Policy  ceases  immediately  after  the  grace  period.  No  death  benefit  is  payable  for deaths occurring after the lapse date. No surrender value or paid-up value exists for term policies. All premiums paid are forfeited.

WHOLE LIFE (VitaGuard) AND ENDOWMENT (VitaSave): If the policy has completed fewer than 3 years of premium payment:  Policy  lapses  with  no  value.  If  the  policy  has  completed  3  or  more  years  of  premium  payment:  Policy automatically converts to a PAID-UP POLICY with a reduced sum assured (paid-up value). Paid-up value = Original sum assured × (Number of premiums paid / Total premiums payable for full term). Bonuses already vested are retained in proportion. No new bonuses accrue on a paid-up policy.

ULIP  (VitaGrow  Plus):  During  the  5-year  lock-in:  If  premium  is  unpaid  after  the  grace  period,  the  policy  enters  a discontinuance period (2 years). Fund value is moved to the Discontinued Policy Fund (DPF) which earns a minimum of 4% p.a. After the discontinuance period (or after lock-in ends): Policyholder may revive or receive DPF value minus discontinuance charges.

## PART 2: REVIVAL (REINSTATEMENT)

## Revival Window and Eligibility

A lapsed policy can be revived within 5 years from the date of the first unpaid premium (or within the policy term, whichever is earlier).

REVIVAL  IS  NOT  POSSIBLE  IF:  The  revival  period  of  5  years  has  expired;  The  life  assured  has  developed  an uninsurable condition (subject to underwriting); The policy was surrendered (surrendered policies cannot be revived).

WHAT REVIVAL REQUIRES: Payment of all outstanding premiums since lapse date, PLUS Interest on outstanding premiums at 9% per annum from due date of each unpaid premium to date of revival payment. A satisfactory health declaration (Form REV-01). For policies lapsed &gt; 2 years: a fresh medical examination (same requirements as new business for age and sum assured). Submission of Form REV-01 (Revival Application) at any VitaLife branch or online.

## Revival Interest Calculation Example

EXAMPLE: Monthly premium: INR 5,000. Policy lapsed in January 2024 (12 premiums unpaid as of January 2025). Interest rate: 9% p.a.

Total unpaid premiums: 12 × INR 5,000 = INR 60,000 Average period outstanding: ~6.5 months » 0.54 years Interest: INR 60,000 × 9% × 0.54 = INR 2,916 (approximate) Total revival payment: INR 60,000 + INR 2,916 = INR 62,916 (approximate)

Exact interest is calculated on each unpaid premium individually from its due date. Use the VitaLife Revival Calculator at vitalife.com/revival for precise amounts.

## Special Revival Schemes

VitaLife periodically offers Special Revival Schemes (SRS) that waive or reduce the interest component to encourage policyholders to revive lapsed policies. When an SRS is active: Normal interest charges are reduced or waived. Health requirements may be relaxed for shorter lapse periods. Check vitalife.com/offers or contact your advisor for current SRS offers.

CAUTION: SRS availability varies and is announced with limited validity periods (typically 3-6 months). Do not delay revival in anticipation of an SRS as the lapse period continues to accumulate.

## PART 3: REDUCED PAID-UP POLICY

## Paid-Up Policy Rules

When  a  participating  plan  (Whole  Life  or  Endowment)  lapses  after  3  or  more  years  of  premium  payment,  it automatically becomes a REDUCED PAID-UP (RPU) policy.

PAID-UP SUM ASSURED FORMULA: Paid-Up SA = Original SA × (n / N) where n = number of premiums actually paid, N = total premiums payable for full policy term.

EXAMPLE: 20-year endowment plan, annual premium. SA = INR 10,00,000. Policyholder paid 6 years (6 premiums) then stopped. Paid-Up SA = 10,00,000 × (6/20) = INR 3,00,000. Death benefit: INR 3,00,000 + proportionate vested bonuses.  Maturity  benefit:  INR  3,00,000  +  proportionate  vested  bonuses  (at  end  of  original  20-year  term  -  no extension).

BONUSES ON PAID-UP POLICY: Reversionary bonuses accrued up to the date of lapse are retained in proportion to the paid-up ratio. No new bonuses accrue on a paid-up policy. The paid-up policy continues in force until the end of the original policy term or the life assured's death, whichever is first.
