## LIFE INSURANCE POLICY - TERMS &amp; CONDITIONS

VitaLife Insurance Corporation | Master Policy Document | 2025

## SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS

## 1.1 Key Definitions

DEATH BENEFIT: The amount payable to the nominee upon the death of the life assured during the policy term.

FREE LOOK PERIOD: A period of 15 days (30 days for policies sold through distance marketing) from the date of receipt of the policy document during which the policyholder may return the policy and receive a refund of premium paid, minus proportionate risk premium and medical examination expenses.

GRACE PERIOD: A period after the premium due date during which the policy remains in force. Grace period: 30 days for  premiums payable annually, semi-annually, or quarterly; 15 days for monthly premiums. Death during the grace period: claim is payable after deduction of outstanding premium.

LAPSE: A policy that has not been revived after the grace period expires. A lapsed policy has no cash value (for term life) or reduced paid-up value (for participating plans) until revived.

LIFE ASSURED: The person whose life is insured under the policy. May be the same as the policyholder or a different person.

MATURITY DATE: The date on  which  the  policy  term  ends  and  the  maturity  benefit  (where  applicable)  becomes payable.

NOMINEE:  The  person(s)  designated  by  the  policyholder  to  receive  the  death  benefit  upon  the  death  of  the  life assured.

PAID-UP POLICY: A policy where the policyholder has stopped paying premiums but the policy remains in force for a reduced sum assured (paid-up value). Applicable to participating plans after at least 3 years of premium payment.

POLICYHOLDER: The person who owns the policy and is responsible for paying premiums.

REVIVAL: The process of reinstating a lapsed policy. Revival is allowed within 5 years of the first unpaid premium date, subject to satisfactory health declaration and payment of all outstanding premiums with interest.

RIDER: An optional add-on benefit that enhances the base policy coverage. Rider premiums are payable in addition to the base policy premium.

SUM ASSURED (SUM AT RISK): The guaranteed amount payable as the death benefit or maturity benefit as specified in the policy schedule.

SURRENDER VALUE: The amount payable to the policyholder  upon  early  termination  of  the  policy.  Equal  to  the higher of Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) or Special Surrender Value (SSV). Applicable only to participating plans (Whole Life and Endowment) and ULIP.

## SECTION 2: POLICY ISSUANCE

## 2.1 Application Process

PROPOSAL FORM: The policyholder must complete VitaLife's standard proposal form truthfully and completely. Any material misrepresentation, non-disclosure, or fraud in the proposal form may result in the policy being declared void.

MEDICAL EXAMINATION: Required based on age,  sum  assured,  and  health  history.  VitaLife  may  waive  medical examination for younger applicants with lower sum assured and clean health history.

UNDERWRITING DECISION: Based on the proposal, medical reports, and risk assessment. Possible outcomes: (a) Standard  rates  -  no  extra  premium  loading;  (b)  Substandard  rates  -  extra  premium  loading  due  to  health, occupational,  or  lifestyle  risk  factors;  (c)  Postponement  -  application  deferred  pending  additional  information;  (d) Decline - application rejected due to unacceptable risk.

POLICY DOCUMENT DELIVERY: Policy document will be delivered within 15 days of acceptance of proposal and receipt of first premium. The policyholder should review all details and inform VitaLife of any discrepancies within 30 days.

## 2.2 Incontestability Clause

After the policy has been in force for 3 consecutive years from the date of commencement (or revival), VitaLife shall not contest  the  validity  of  the  policy  on  the  grounds  of  misrepresentation  or  non-disclosure  of  material  facts,  except  in cases of fraud.

FRAUD EXCEPTION: VitaLife may contest a policy at any time on the grounds of proven fraudulent misrepresentation, even after 3 years.

## SECTION 3: PREMIUM PAYMENT

## 3.1 Premium Payment Terms

PAYMENT METHODS: Premium may be paid via NACH (auto-debit), online banking, UPI, cheque, or demand draft payable to VitaLife Insurance Corporation.

PREMIUM RECEIPT: A premium  receipt  will  be  issued  for  every  premium  payment.  Premium  payment  history  is available on the VitaLife policyholder portal at vitalife.com/myaccount.

REINSTATEMENT AFTER GRACE PERIOD LAPSE: If the premium is not paid within the grace period, the policy lapses. For participating plans (Whole Life, Endowment): the policy may convert to a reduced paid-up policy (if 3+ years of premiums paid) or lapse without value. For term life: no survival benefit; policy lapses with no value.

## 3.2 Premium Loading for Substandard Lives

Applicants  assessed  as  substandard  risks  will  be  charged  an  extra  premium  over  the  standard  tabular  rate.  Extra premium  loading  categories:  Class  I  (Low  Risk):  +25%  of  standard  premium  Class  II  (Moderate  Risk):  +50%  of standard premium Class III (High Risk): +100% of standard premium Class IV (Very High Risk): +150% of standard premium or postponement/decline

Examples of conditions attracting extra premium loading: - Controlled hypertension (well-managed): Class I - Type 2 diabetes  (well-controlled,  HbA1c  &lt;  8%):  Class  II  -  Obesity  (BMI  35-39.9):  Class  I-II  depending  on  other  factors  History  of  cancer  (5+  years  in  remission):  Class  II-III  depending  on  type  -  High-risk  occupation  (mining,  oil  &amp;  gas, armed forces): Class I-II

## SECTION 4: EXCLUSIONS

## 4.1 Standard Exclusions - All Plans

The following exclusions apply to all VitaLife policies. Death resulting from these causes will NOT be covered:

- (a) SUICIDE: Death by suicide within 12 months of policy commencement or revival. (Note: After 12 months, suicide is covered in accordance with IRDAI regulations.)
- (b)  HAZARDOUS  ACTIVITIES:  Death  while  participating  in  aviation  other  than  as  a  fare-paying  passenger  on  a licensed commercial airline; motor racing; mountaineering above 6,000 metres; skydiving; bungee jumping; deep-sea diving.
- (c) SELF-INFLICTED INJURY: Death resulting from intentional self-harm (not meeting suicide definition).
- (d) CRIMINAL ACTS: Death while committing or attempting to commit a cognisable criminal offence.
- (e) WAR AND CIVIL UNREST: Death directly caused by war (declared or undeclared), invasion, rebellion, or military action.
- (f) NUCLEAR HAZARD: Death caused by nuclear fission, fusion, contamination, or radioactive fallout.
- (g)  INTOXICATION:  Death  where  blood  alcohol  level  exceeds  legal  limits  or  where  the  deceased  was  under  the influence of illegal drugs at the time of death.
- (h)  HIV/AIDS:  Death  resulting  directly  from  HIV/AIDS  (applies  only  to  policies  issued  before  April  1,  2020;  policies issued after this date cover HIV/AIDS in accordance with IRDAI circular LI/MISC/142).

NOTE ON WAITING PERIODS: Certain critical illness riders have a 90-day waiting period from policy inception; CI claims during this period are not payable.

NOTE  ON  ACCIDENTAL  DEATH  BENEFIT  RIDER:  The  ADB  rider  exclusions  additionally  include  death  due  to self-administered substances (including overdose of prescription medication), death while insane, and death due to bacterial infection (unless resulting from an accidental cut or wound).

## SECTION 5: CLAIMS PROCEDURES

## 5.1 Death Claim Process

STEP 1 - INTIMATION: Notify VitaLife Claims Department as soon as possible after the death of the life assured. Intimation  can  be  given  at  any  VitaLife  branch,  online  at  vitalife.com/claims,  or  by  calling  1-800-VL-CLAIM (1-800-855-2524).

STEP 2 - CLAIM FORM: Download and complete the Death Claim Form (DCF-01) from vitalife.com/claims. The form must be signed by the nominee(s).

STEP  3  -  DOCUMENTATION:  Submit  the  following  documents:  Mandatory:  Original  policy  document;  Death certificate  (certified  copy  from  local  authority);  Death  claim  form  (DCF-01);  Nominee's  photo  ID  and  address  proof; Nominee's  bank  account  details  (cancelled  cheque  or  bank  passbook  copy).  Additional  for  non-natural  deaths: Post-mortem report; Police FIR (if applicable); Panchnama; Medical examiner's certificate. Additional for accidental death claims: Police FIR; Motor accident report (if vehicle accident); Newspaper clipping (if major accident).

STEP 4 - INVESTIGATION: VitaLife may appoint a claim investigator for claims within 3 years of policy issuance, claims above INR 50,00,000, or claims where cause of death is unclear.

STEP 5 - SETTLEMENT: Once all documents are received and verified: Early death claims (within 3 years): Settled within 30 days of receipt of all documents. All other claims: Settled within 30 days.

STEP 6 - PAYMENT: Death benefit paid directly to nominee's bank account via NEFT/RTGS. If the nominee is a minor, payment is made to the appointee named in the policy.

## 5.2 Maturity Claim Process

Maturity claims (Endowment, Whole Life, ULIP) are processed automatically by VitaLife: 30 days before maturity date: VitaLife  sends  a  Maturity  Intimation  Letter  to  the  registered  address.  15  days  before  maturity  date:  Policyholder submits: Original policy document; Maturity claim discharge form; Bank account details. On maturity date: Payment credited to policyholder's bank account by NEFT/RTGS.

## SECTION 6: POLICY LOANS

## 6.1 Loan Against Policy

ELIGIBILITY: Policy must have acquired a surrender value (typically after 3 years of premium payment for participating plans; after 5 years for ULIP).

MAXIMUM LOAN AMOUNT: Whole Life / Endowment: Up to 85% of surrender value ULIP: Up to 40% of fund value

INTEREST  RATE:  Prevailing  VitaLife  policy  loan  rate,  currently  9.5%  per  annum,  compounded  annually.  Interest accrues from the date of loan disbursement.

REPAYMENT: No fixed repayment schedule. Outstanding loan + accrued interest will be deducted from the claim amount (death benefit, maturity benefit, or surrender value) if not repaid.

FORECLOSURE: If outstanding loan + interest equals or exceeds the surrender value at any time, VitaLife will send a 30-day  notice.  If  not  repaid  within  30  days,  the  policy  will  be  foreclosed  (terminated)  and  any  residual  amount (surrender value minus loan) will be paid.

## SECTION 7: NOMINATION AND ASSIGNMENT

## 7.1 Nomination

A  nominee  is  entitled  to  receive  the  death  benefit  if  the  life  assured  dies  during  the  policy  term.  BENEFICIAL NOMINEE (Section  39  of  Insurance  Act  1938):  Spouse,  children,  and  parents  named  as  nominees  are  beneficial nominees and have an absolute right to the policy proceeds. No other claimant (including creditors) can claim the proceeds. OTHER NOMINEES: Any other person may be named as nominee; however, they receive proceeds as trustees for the legal heirs of the life assured.

NOMINATION OF MINOR: If nominee is a minor, an appointee (an adult) must be named. The appointee holds the proceeds in trust for the minor until majority.

CHANGE  OF  NOMINATION:  The  policyholder  may  change  the  nominee  at  any  time  during  the  policy  term  by submitting a Nomination Change Request (Form NOM-02) to any VitaLife branch or online.

MULTIPLE NOMINEES: Multiple nominees may be named with specified percentage shares. Shares must total 100%. If a nominee predeceases the life assured, their share passes equally to surviving nominees.

## 7.2 Assignment

The policyholder may assign the policy to another person or entity (e.g., as collateral for a bank loan). Assignment must be in writing on VitaLife's Assignment Form (Form ASN-01) and submitted for endorsement. Types: Absolute Assignment  -  transfers  all  rights  to  the  assignee  permanently;  Conditional  Assignment  -  transfers  rights  under specific conditions (e.g., lender recovers outstanding loan; remaining benefit goes back to nominee).

A policy with a registered assignment cannot be surrendered or modified without the assignee's consent.
