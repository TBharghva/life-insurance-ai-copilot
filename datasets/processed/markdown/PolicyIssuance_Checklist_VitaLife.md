## COMMON REASONS FOR ISSUANCE DELAY

| Reason for Delay                                                | Prevention / Resolution                                                     |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Incomplete proposal form -missing fields or unsigned sections   | Review all pages before submission; agent to countersign all corrections    |
| Illegible medical reports from non-empanelled labs              | Use only VitaLife-empanelled diagnostic centres; resend if reports unclear  |
| Income proof not matching proposed sum assured                  | Submit ITR + salary slip; ensure sum assured within income multiples        |
| Nominee details incomplete -missing appointee for minor nominee | Complete Form NOM-01 with full appointee details before submission          |
| Outstanding medical query from underwriting                     | Respond within 15 days; application lapses after 30 days of no response     |
| Adverse medical history requiring specialist opinion            | Submit specialist report proactively to avoid underwriting delay            |
| First premium cheque dishonoured                                | Ensure adequate funds; prefer NACH mandate or online payment                |
| PAN not linked with Aadhaar (CBDT mandate)                      | Complete PAN-Aadhaar linking at incometax.gov.in before proposal submission |
