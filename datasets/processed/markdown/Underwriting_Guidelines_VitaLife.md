## UNDERWRITING GUIDELINES MANUAL

VitaLife Insurance Corporation | Underwriting Department | 2025 Edition

This  manual  provides  the  underwriting  criteria  and  risk  classification  guidelines  used  by  VitaLife  to  assess  life insurance  applications.  Participants  should  use  this  document  to  simulate  underwriting  risk  assessment  in  the  AI copilot.

## PART 4: FINANCIAL UNDERWRITING (INSURABLE INTEREST)

VitaLife  applies  financial  underwriting  to  ensure  the  sum  assured  is  proportionate  to  the  insurable  interest  of  the applicant.  The  maximum  sum  assured  is  determined  by  the  applicant's  annual  income  and  existing  life  insurance coverage.

| Age Band   | Maximum Sum Assured as Multiple of Annual Income   | Existing Insurance Deducted?                              |
|------------|----------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| 18-35      | Up to 30x annual income                            | Yes - all existing coverage deducted from allowable limit |
| 36-45      | Up to 20x annual income                            | Yes                                                       |
| 46-55      | Up to 15x annual income                            | Yes                                                       |
| 56-65      | Up to 10x annual income                            | Yes                                                       |
