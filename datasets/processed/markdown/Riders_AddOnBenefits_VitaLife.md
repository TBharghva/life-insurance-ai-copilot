VitaLife Insurance Corporation | 2025 Edition | All Products

Riders are optional add-on benefits that can be attached to a VitaLife base policy to provide enhanced protection. Rider premiums  are  charged  separately  and  are  in  addition  to  the  base  policy  premium.  Riders  are  available  at  policy inception or, in some cases, at policy anniversary.

## CRITICAL ILLNESS (CI) RIDER (VL-CIR-2025)

## Coverage

Pays an additional lump sum (rider sum assured) upon first diagnosis of any of the 36 listed critical illnesses. Payment is made irrespective of actual medical expenses incurred and does not affect the base policy death benefit.

Major  organ  transplant  (heart,  liver,  lung, kidney, pancreas) 7. Aorta graft surgery 8. Primary pulmonary arterial hypertension 9. Multiple sclerosis with persisting symptoms  10.  End-stage  liver  disease  11.  End-stage  lung  disease  12.  Motor  neurone  disease  with  permanent symptoms 13. Alzheimer's disease / dementia (before age 65) 14. Parkinson's disease (before age 65) 15. Aplastic anaemia  16.  Muscular  dystrophy  17.  Loss  of  speech  (permanent)  18.  Blindness  (permanent  and  irreversible)  19. Deafness (permanent, both ears) 20. Loss of limbs (at least two limbs) 21. Major burns ( ‡ 20% body surface) 22. Coma of specified severity 23. Benign brain tumour 24. Bacterial meningitis resulting in permanent neurological deficit 25. Viral encephalitis resulting in permanent neurological deficit 26. Fulminant hepatitis 27. HIV due to blood transfusion 28. ## Sum Assured

Minimum: INR 2,00,000. Maximum: INR 1 crore (or base sum assured, whichever is lower).

## Waiting Period

90 days from policy inception. CI claims during the waiting period are not payable. Waiting period does not apply for CI resulting from accidental bodily injury.

## Premium

Based on age, gender, smoking status, and rider sum assured. Indicative rate: INR 15-40 per INR 1,000 CI rider SA per annum (varies by age band).

## ACCIDENTAL DEATH BENEFIT (ADB) RIDER (VL-ADB-2025)

## Coverage

Pays  an  additional  lump  sum  (rider  sum  assured)  if  the  life  assured's  death  is  caused  directly  and  solely  by  an accident, within 180 days of the accident.

## Definition of Accident

An unforeseen, involuntary, sudden event caused by external, violent, and visible means, which results in bodily injury and subsequent death. Road accidents, rail accidents, accidental falls, drowning, and occupational accidents qualify.

## Exclusions

Death by suicide or self-inflicted injury; death due to war or riot; death while under influence of alcohol or drugs; death while participating in criminal activities; death due to pre-existing illness that is accelerated by an accident (death must be SOLELY due to the accident); aviation (other than as fare-paying passenger on licensed commercial carrier).

## Sum Assured

Minimum: INR 2,00,000. Maximum: INR 2 crore (or base sum assured, whichever is lower).

## Premium

Indicative rate: INR 1.50-3.00 per INR 1,000 ADB rider SA per annum (varies by age and occupation class).

## WAIVER OF PREMIUM (WOP) RIDER (VL-WOP-2025)

## Coverage

If the policyholder suffers permanent total disability (PTD) due to accident or illness, all future base policy AND rider premiums are waived for the remainder of the premium payment term. The policy continues in full force without further premium payment.

## Definition of PTD for WOP

Permanent and irreversible inability to perform at least 3 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) without assistance, or permanent  and  complete  inability  to  work  in  any  occupation  for  remuneration.  ADLs:  bathing,  dressing,  feeding, transferring (moving from bed to chair), continence, and toileting.

## Premium Waiver Scope

Waives both base policy premium AND premiums for all attached riders. Policy continues with all benefits intact.

## Premium

Indicative rate: 10-15% of base policy premium per annum.

## PERMANENT TOTAL DISABILITY (PTD) RIDER (VL-PTD-2025)

## Coverage

Pays the rider sum assured as a lump sum if the life assured suffers PTD due to accident or illness, permanently and irreversibly preventing them from working in any occupation for remuneration. Available on Term Life and Whole Life products only.

## Sum Assured

Minimum: INR 2,00,000. Maximum: INR 50,00,000.

## Premium

Indicative rate: INR 5-12 per INR 1,000 PTD rider SA per annum (varies by age and occupation).
