## Annuity

A financial  product  where  the  policyholder  pays  a  lump  sum  or  series  of  payments,  and  in  return  receives  regular income payments, either for life  or  for  a  fixed  period.  VitaLife  offers  immediate  and  deferred  annuity  products  (not covered in this dataset).

## Assignment

The  legal  transfer  of  the  policyholder's  rights  and  interests  in  a  life  insurance  policy  to  another  person  or  entity. Absolute  assignment  transfers  all  rights  permanently;  conditional  assignment  transfers  rights  subject  to  specified conditions.

## Beneficial Nominee

A nominee who is a spouse, child, or parent of the life assured. A beneficial nominee has an absolute right to the policy proceeds on the life assured's death and is protected from claims by creditors and other legal heirs.

## Bonus (Reversionary)

An addition to the sum assured, declared annually by the insurer as a percentage of the sum assured, for participating (with-profits) policies. Once declared, it cannot be taken away. It is paid along with the sum assured at maturity or death.

## Bonus (Terminal)

A one-time bonus declared by VitaLife at the time of policy termination (death, maturity, or surrender) in addition to reversionary bonuses. Not guaranteed; depends on fund performance.

## Death Benefit

The  amount  paid  to  the  nominee(s)  upon  the  death  of  the  life  assured  during  the  policy  term.  For  term  life:  sum assured. For participating plans: sum assured plus accrued bonuses. For ULIP: higher of fund value or sum assured.

## Free Look Period

A period of 15 days (30 days for policies sold online or by direct marketing) from the date of receipt of the policy document, during which the policyholder may return the policy for a full refund of premium, minus proportionate risk premium and medical costs.

## Fund Value (ULIP)

The current market value of units held in a ULIP policy, calculated by multiplying the number of units by the current Net Asset Value (NAV) of the fund.

## Grace Period

A period after the premium due date during which the policy remains in force without lapse. VitaLife grace period: 30 days for annual/semi-annual/quarterly premiums; 15 days for monthly premiums.

## Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV)

The  minimum  surrender  value  guaranteed  by  the  policy.  GSV  =  30%  of  total  premiums  paid  (excluding  first-year premium and rider premiums) after the policy has been in force for 3 years. GSV increases over time.

## IRDAI

Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India - the statutory body regulating the insurance industry in India. VitaLife is licensed and regulated by IRDAI.

## Lapse

The termination of a policy due to non-payment of premium after the grace period. A lapsed term policy has no value; a lapsed participating plan converts to paid-up (if 3+ years premiums paid) or lapses without value.

## Maturity Benefit

The benefit paid to the policyholder (life assured) when the policy term ends and the life assured is alive. Applicable to endowment and whole life plans and ULIP. Term life has no maturity benefit.

## Mortality Charge

A monthly deduction in ULIP to provide the life insurance coverage. Calculated based on the 'sum at risk' (sum assured minus fund value) and the life assured's age. Increases with age.

## NAV (Net Asset Value)

The per-unit value of a ULIP fund, calculated daily as (Total fund assets minus liabilities) / Number of units outstanding. ULIP premiums are allocated as units at the prevailing NAV on the date of allocation.

## Nomination

The process by which the policyholder designates a person (nominee) to receive the death benefit if the life assured dies during the policy term.

## Paid-Up Policy

A policy that has been in force for at least 3 years (for participating plans) and for which no further premiums will be paid, but which continues in force for a reduced sum assured (paid-up value) until the end of the original policy term.

## Policy Loan

A loan taken against the surrender value of a life insurance policy. VitaLife allows loans of up to 85% of surrender value for participating plans and 40% of fund value for ULIPs. Outstanding loan plus interest is deducted from the claim or maturity amount.

## Premium Loading (Extra Premium)

An  additional  premium  charged  over  the  standard  tabular  rate  for  applicants  assessed  as  substandard  health  or occupational risks. Expressed as a percentage of the standard premium (e.g., +25%, +50%).

## Revival

The reinstatement of a lapsed policy by paying all outstanding premiums with interest and submitting a satisfactory health declaration. Revival must occur within 5 years of the first unpaid premium date.

## Rider

An optional add-on benefit attached to a base life insurance policy for additional coverage. Common riders: Critical Illness, Accidental Death Benefit, Waiver of Premium, Permanent Total Disability.

## Section 80C

A section of the Indian Income Tax Act allowing deduction of life insurance premiums paid from taxable income, up to INR 1,50,000 per year.

## Section 10(10D)

A section of the Indian Income Tax Act exempting death and maturity proceeds of life insurance policies from income tax, subject to conditions (premium must be £ 10% of sum assured for policies issued after April 1, 2012).

## Subrogation

Not  applicable  in  life  insurance  (applicable  in  general  insurance).  Life  insurance  death  benefits  are  not  subject  to subrogation.

## Sum Assured

The guaranteed amount payable as the death benefit (and, for endowment/whole life, the maturity benefit). Chosen by the policyholder at inception.

## Surrender Value

The amount payable when a policyholder voluntarily terminates the policy before its maturity. Equal to the higher of the Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) or Special Surrender Value (SSV). Surrendering early typically results in a loss relative to premiums paid.

## ULIP (Unit-Linked Insurance Plan)

A life insurance product combining insurance protection with market-linked investment. Premiums are split between risk cover (mortality charge) and investment in market funds of the policyholder's choice.

## Underwriting

The process by which VitaLife assesses the risk of insuring an applicant, based on health, age, occupation, lifestyle, and financial profile, to determine eligibility and premium rate.
