## VITASHIELD TERM LIFE PLAN

Maximum Protection. Minimum Premium.

## Product Overview

VitaShield Term Life is a pure protection plan that provides a high sum assured at an affordable premium. If the life assured dies during the policy term, the full sum assured is paid to the nominee. There is no maturity benefit - if the life assured survives the policy term, no amount is payable. This makes term life the most cost-effective way to provide income replacement and financial security for dependents.

## Key Features

COVERAGE AMOUNTS: Minimum sum assured: INR  25,00,000  (25  lakhs).  Maximum:  No  upper  limit  (subject  to underwriting).

POLICY TERMS: 10, 15, 20, 25, or 30 years. Entry age: 18-65 years. Maximum maturity age: 75 years.

PREMIUM  PAYMENT:  Level  premium  (same  throughout  the  policy  term).  Single  pay  option  available.  Annual, semi-annual,  quarterly,  or  monthly  payment  frequency.  Grace  period:  30  days  for  annual/semi-annual/quarterly;  15 days for monthly.

PREMIUM FACTORS: Age, gender, sum assured, policy term, smoking status, occupation, medical history, and BMI. Women receive a 5% premium discount. Non-smokers receive a 7.5% premium discount.

DEATH BENEFIT: 100% of sum assured paid to nominee as lump sum. Option to receive benefit as monthly income (10 years) instead of lump sum. Return of Premium (ROP) variant available: full premiums returned at end of term (at higher premium).

RIDERS  AVAILABLE:  Critical  Illness  Rider  (CI):  Additional  sum  assured  if  diagnosed  with  listed  critical  illness. Accidental Death Benefit Rider (ADB): Additional sum assured if death is accidental. Waiver of Premium Rider (WOP): Future premiums waived if policyholder becomes permanently disabled. Permanent Total Disability Rider (PTD): Sum assured paid if policyholder suffers PTD.

## Suitability

BEST SUITED FOR: Young earners with dependents who need maximum coverage at minimum cost; Home loan or business loan borrowers who want loan coverage; Primary breadwinners with limited insurance budget; Those who have other investment vehicles (PPF, mutual funds) for savings.

NOT SUITABLE FOR: Those seeking a savings or investment component; Those wanting a guaranteed return or maturity payout; Senior applicants (over 60) where premium costs escalate significantly; Those wanting a plan to leave behind wealth (consider Whole Life instead).

## VITAGUARD WHOLE LIFE PLAN

Protection for Life. Savings for Legacy.

## Product Overview

VitaGuard Whole Life is a participating (with-profits) life insurance plan providing coverage for the whole of life (up to age 99) combined with a cash value accumulation feature. The plan pays a death benefit to nominees whenever the life  assured  dies  -  there  is  no  expiry  of  coverage.  The  plan  also  accumulates  cash  value  through  reversionary bonuses declared annually by VitaLife.

## Key Features

COVERAGE AMOUNTS: Minimum sum assured: INR 5,00,000. Maximum: Subject to underwriting. Entry age: 0 (28 days) to 60 years. Coverage continues to age 99.

PREMIUM PAYMENT OPTIONS: Regular pay: Premiums paid for the entire policy term. Limited pay: Premiums paid for 10, 15, or 20 years; coverage continues for life. Single pay: One-time premium. Note: Limited pay and single pay have higher per-instalment premiums.

CASH  VALUE  &amp;  SURRENDER  VALUE:  Cash  value  begins  accumulating  after  policy  year  3.  Surrender  value  = Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) or Special Surrender Value (SSV), whichever is higher. GSV: 30% of premiums paid (excluding first year and rider premiums) after 3 years; increases to 50% after year 7 and 90% after year 10. Policy loan available: up to 85% of surrender value.

BONUSES: Reversionary Bonus: Declared annually as a percentage of sum assured; once added, cannot be taken away.  Terminal  Bonus:  Paid  at  maturity,  death,  or  surrender  (at  VitaLife's  discretion  based  on  fund  performance). Current indicative reversionary bonus: 4.2% per annum of sum assured (not guaranteed; based on 2024 performance).

MATURITY BENEFIT: If  the  life  assured  survives  to  age  99:  Sum  Assured  +  all  accrued  reversionary  bonuses  + terminal bonus is paid.

## Suitability

BEST  SUITED  FOR:  Individuals  seeking  lifelong  coverage  with  no  risk  of  outliving  the  policy;  Estate  planning  leaving a legacy for heirs; Those who want forced savings alongside protection; Business partners needing key person or buy-sell agreement coverage; Parents seeking coverage that can be transferred to children.

NOT SUITABLE FOR: Those seeking high  investment  returns  (ULIP  or  mutual  funds  outperform);  Young  earners needing maximum coverage at minimum cost (term life is better); Those with a short investment horizon (surrender value in early years is low).

## VITASAVE ENDOWMENT PLAN

Save. Protect. Achieve Your Goals.

## Product Overview

VitaSave  Endowment  is  a  participating  savings-cum-protection  plan.  It  combines  life  insurance  coverage  with  a guaranteed savings goal: the policy pays the sum assured plus accumulated bonuses either on death (to nominees) or on  survival  to  the  end  of  the  policy  term  (to  the  policyholder).  This  dual-purpose  structure  makes  it  suitable  for goal-based saving - children's education, marriage, home purchase, or retirement.

## Key Features

COVERAGE AMOUNTS: Minimum sum assured: INR 1,00,000. Maximum: No upper limit (subject to underwriting). Entry age: 0 (91 days) to 60 years. Maturity age: Maximum 70 years.

POLICY TERMS: 10, 15, 20, or 25 years (fixed term - not whole of life). Premium payment: Level throughout the term. Single pay option available for 10-year plans.

MATURITY BENEFIT (Survival Benefit): If the life assured survives to the end of the policy term: Sum Assured + all accrued  reversionary  bonuses  +  terminal  bonus  is  paid.  This  is  the  policyholder's  savings  goal  payout.  Current indicative maturity bonus: 4.0% per annum reversionary bonus (not guaranteed).

DEATH BENEFIT: Sum assured + all accrued bonuses paid to nominee. In the early years of the policy, total death benefit may be higher than surrender value.

SURRENDER VALUE: Available  from  year  3  onwards.  Similar  GSV/SSV  structure  as  Whole  Life.  Surrendering  a participating endowment plan early forfeits a significant portion of accumulated bonuses.

LOAN FACILITY: Policy loan available after year 3: up to 85% of surrender value at prevailing VitaLife lending rate (currently 9.5% p.a. simple interest).

## Suitability

BEST SUITED FOR: Goal-based  savers  with  a  defined  financial  target  and  timeline  (e.g.,  child's  education  in  15 years); Conservative investors who prefer guaranteed returns with protection; Those who want a disciplined savings vehicle with a safety net; Applicants who are not eligible for high-return instruments due to risk aversion.

NOT  SUITABLE  FOR:  Those  seeking  flexible  premium  or  investment  options  (ULIP  is  better);  High  net-worth individuals with sophisticated investment portfolios (returns may lag); Those with a need for high pure coverage at low cost (term life provides more coverage per rupee).

## VITAGROW PLUS - ULIP

Grow Your Wealth. Protect Your Future.

## Product Overview

VitaGrow  Plus  is  a  Unit-Linked  Insurance  Plan  (ULIP)  that  combines  life  insurance  protection  with  market-linked investment. A portion of the premium goes towards life cover; the remainder is invested in funds of the policyholder's choice (equity, debt, or balanced). The death benefit is the higher of the fund value or the sum assured, providing a safety net regardless of market performance.

## Key Features

COVERAGE  AMOUNTS:  Minimum  sum  assured:  10x  annualised  premium.  Maximum:  40x  annualised  premium (higher  subject  to  underwriting  and  age).  Entry  age:  0  (91  days)  to  65  years.  Maximum  maturity  age:  75  years. Minimum policy term: 10 years. Lock-in: 5 years (IRDAI-mandated; no withdrawals before year 5).

FUND  OPTIONS:  VitaGrow  Equity  Fund:  80-100%  equity;  high  risk/high  return;  benchmark:  Nifty  50.  VitaGrow Balanced Fund: 40-60% equity, remainder in debt; moderate risk. VitaGrow Debt Fund: 0-20% equity, 80-100% debt instruments; low risk. VitaGrow Liquid Fund: 100% money market instruments; capital preservation. VitaGrow Dynamic Fund: Actively managed asset allocation; fund manager discretion.

CHARGES: Premium Allocation Charge (PAC): Year 1: 5%, Year 2: 4%, Year 3+: 2%. Policy Administration Charge (PAC):  Rs.  60/month  (Year  1-5);  Rs.  40/month  (Year  6+).  Fund  Management  Charge  (FMC):  Equity:  1.35%  p.a.; Balanced: 1.10% p.a.; Debt/Liquid: 0.75% p.a. Mortality Charge (MC): Deducted monthly from fund value based on age and sum at risk. Surrender Charge (SC): Year 1-5: Not applicable (locked in); Year 6: 2%; Year 7: 1%; Year 8+: Nil.

PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS: Allowed after the 5-year lock-in. Minimum withdrawal: Rs. 5,000. Maximum: Up to 20% of fund value per year. Fund value must remain above 105% of sum assured after withdrawal.

SWITCHING BETWEEN FUNDS: 4 free switches per policy year. Additional switches: Rs. 250 per switch.

DEATH BENEFIT: Higher of (a) fund value at time of death, or (b) sum assured minus all partial withdrawals in last 2 years. Death claim settled within 30 days of submission of all documents.

## Suitability

BEST SUITED FOR: Long-term investors (10+ year horizon) who want market-linked growth; Individuals comfortable with equity market volatility; Those who want flexibility to change investment allocation; Investors who want insurance and investment in one product for simplicity; Young professionals in the accumulation phase of wealth.

NOT SUITABLE FOR: Risk-averse individuals seeking guaranteed returns; Short-term investors (the 5-year lock-in and  high  early  charges  make  short-term  exits  expensive);  Those  with  purely  protection  needs  (term  life  is  more cost-effective for coverage); Senior applicants (high mortality charges reduce investment corpus).
