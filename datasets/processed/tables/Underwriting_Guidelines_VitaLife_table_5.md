| Risk Class               | Description                                                   | Typical Occupations                                                                                                       | Extra Loading            |
|--------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Class A (Standard)       | Low-risk, sedentary or light work                             | Accountant, teacher, doctor, software engineer, banker, manager, clerk                                                    | Nil                      |
| Class B (Low Risk)       | Moderate physical activity; low injury risk                   | Nurse, pharmacist, salesperson, photographer, real estate agent, technician (indoor)                                      | Nil - +10%               |
| Class C (Medium Risk)    | Regular exposure to physical risk or hazardous environment    | Driver (light commercial), electrician, plumber, construction supervisor, farmer, fireman (administrative)                | +25% to +50%             |
| Class D (High Risk)      | Significant physical danger; injury or fatality risk elevated | Mining worker, oil & gas worker, soldier/sailor/airforce (non-combat), fisherman, logging worker, structural steel worker | +50% to +75%             |
| Class E (Very High Risk) | Extreme physical danger; regular life risk exposure           | Combat military, active bomb disposal, test pilot, offshore oil rig worker, deep-sea diver                                | +75% to +150% or Decline |
